- **Non-constant λ with >1 LE is NOT uniform (≠ PSV):** perturbing λ_i moves the distribution away from the uniform PSV (TV > 0.01) — confirming PASV genuinely generalizes PSV rather than collapsing to it.
- **The |max_≺(Sₜ)| factor is essential (Remark 3.3):** the naive WSV formula (without the numerator |max| factor) applied to general posets would *not* reduce to PSV under const-λ; the factor is required for Proposition 3.1. (Our PASV with the factor reduces exactly.)
